/ THE STORY

About Musicated.

Musicated Records is a UK independent label and events collective creating various things for rave culture.

Founded by UK-based artist Eric Martin (Technotronic, Me One), Musicated is a deep, soulful house and hip-hop label for the people.

Today, the family includes producers and vocalists across the UK, Europe and the US. The label has scored multiple Traxsource Garage House #1s, supported artists into broadcast rotation, and built a sync arm that places music with Sony/EMI and BMG for TV and film clients.
