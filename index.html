/ FOR RAVE CULTURE

A record label and an events collective.

Musicated is a UK independent label and an events collective creating various things for rave culture — records, parties, radio shows, sync.

We also score and place music across genres for TV and Film. Our broadcaster clients include Sony/EMI and BMG. Our dynamic roster of artists and producers sit at the forefront of the contemporary soulful house movement.
